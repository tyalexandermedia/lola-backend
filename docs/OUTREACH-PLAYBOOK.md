## The one motion (don't overcomplicate it)

**Run their free Growth Score → send them the result → invite them to a 15-min call.**

That's it. You lead with value (a real diagnostic of their business), not a pitch.
The score opens the door; the call closes. You are *not* cold-pitching $497 — you're
offering a free look, then a free call.

**Daily minimum to hit 2 clients fast:** 5 Growth Scores run + 5 outreach messages
sent, every weekday. ~25 touches/week. At a normal 10–20% reply→call rate that's
3–5 calls/week; you need ~4–8 good calls to close 2. So this is a **1–2 week** play
if you do the reps.

## Who to target (in this order)

1. **Warm — people who already know you.** Contractors you've met, gym/church/
   network connections, past leads, anyone who's seen your stuff. Start here. These
   close fastest.
2. **Semi-warm — local Tampa Bay trades you can reference a mutual.** "Saw you
   through [person/group]."
3. **Cold — local service businesses with an obviously weak Google presence.** Soft
   wash, pressure washing, plumbing, HVAC, roofing, pool service. Find them: search
   "[trade] [city]" on Google/Maps and look for the ones ranking page 2+, no reviews,
   ugly or no website. Those are your best Growth Score demos — the gaps are real.

**Territory rule (it's true, use it):** one client per trade per area. Once you sign a
Clearwater soft-wash company, you can't take their competitor — say that, it's honest
and it creates real reason to move now (no fake "3 spots left" countdown — ever).

## The 15-minute Growth Call (how to close)

Keep it tight. Structure:

1. **(2 min) Their world.** "Where do most of your jobs come from right now? Where
   do you *wish* they came from?" Let them talk.
2. **(5 min) Walk the score.** Share their Growth Score. Point at the 2 biggest gaps
   and what each one costs in plain terms ("when someone searches '[trade] [city]' at
   10pm, you don't show up — that call goes to whoever does"). Don't invent numbers.
3. **(3 min) The system.** "Here's how I'd fix it — the Lola Local Growth System. One
   price, done for you: website built in, Google Business Profile managed, missed-call
   text-back, follow-up, reviews, tracking. $497/month plus a one-time setup from $497.
   90-day initial term, then month-to-month. One client per trade per area."
4. **(2 min) Honest guardrail.** "I don't promise a number of leads — nobody honest
   can. I promise we do the work: we finish the agreed launch scope or keep working at
   no extra charge until it's done." (This builds trust and separates you from every
   agency they've been burned by.)
5. **(3 min) Close.** "Want me to grab your territory before someone in [city] does?"
   → If yes: confirm scope + territory in writing, send the setup invoice, start.
   → If "need to think": "Totally fair. The score and the fixes are yours regardless.
   Want me to hold your trade/area for 48 hours while you decide?"

## Objection responses (honest, no hard sell)

- **"$497 is a lot."** → "One extra booked job a month usually more than covers it —
  and the website's built in, which most shops charge $3,000+ for alone. It's
  month-to-month after 90 days; if it's not working, you're not stuck."
- **"I do my own marketing."** → "Then the free Growth Score will either confirm you're
  dialed in — great — or show you two quick fixes. Either way you win. Want it?"
- **"No time."** → "That's the whole point — it's done for you. You answer the phone; I
  handle the ranking."
- **"Can you guarantee leads / page 1?"** → "No, and anyone who does is guessing.
  Competition, reviews and how fast you answer decide that. What I guarantee is the
  work gets done. That's the honest version."
- **"Let me think about it."** → "Of course. Keep the fixes. Want me to hold your
  territory 48 hours so a competitor doesn't take the slot while you decide?"

Keep a simple sheet (name · trade · city · score sent? · replied? · call booked? ·
closed?). The only metric that matters right now is **booked calls → clients.** Ignore
traffic. When you've got your first 2, we wire Phase B (payment-triggered onboarding +
turning on the automated nurture emails) so this runs with less of your time.

## The rules (non-negotiable, they're the brand)

- Never fabricate a result, a stat, a review, or a "client got X."
- Never promise leads, revenue, or rankings.
