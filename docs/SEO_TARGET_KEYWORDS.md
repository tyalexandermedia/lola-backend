# SEO target-keyword map — Lola / marketing services

The one-page answer to "what are we trying to rank for, and which page owns
each?" Built from the pages that already exist. Ranked by **ROI = intent ×
winnability**, not by raw volume — for a local service, a buyer typing a
competitor's name is worth more than a thousand tire-kickers.

> This map names intent and assigns owners. It does **not** invent search
> volumes — validate the head terms in a keyword tool (or your own Growth Score)
> before building, and drop any with no real intent. That validation is your
> "week 1, pick your money keywords" step, run on yourself.

## The one focus (say no to everything else)

**Local SEO + AI-search visibility for home-service contractors, Tampa Bay.**
One topical cluster. Every page reinforces that entity. Fitness/wellness is
**ethos on `/about`** — never its own keyword cluster. A fitness audience and a
contractor audience share almost no keywords; chasing both makes the domain rank
for neither (this is the BRAND-STRATEGY thesis, and it holds).

## Winnability (why this order)

- **Win the long tail first.** `[trade] + [city]` and `[competitor] alternative` are low-competition, high-intent — you can rank these in weeks. Head terms like "local SEO" are years of authority; don't lead with them.
- **`/vs` before `/lp`** for *speed to revenue* (buyers are further along); **`/lp` before `/vs`** for *volume*. You said best-ROI → we're doing `/vs` expansion first.

## Baseline before you build (your step)

Pick your **top 10 target queries** from Tiers 1–2, record today's rank for each
(a tool, or your own Growth Score on the site), and re-check monthly. That's how
the climb becomes provable — and provable ranking is your best sales asset.
