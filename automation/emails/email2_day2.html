Getting found is only half the job.

Most local service businesses in {{city}} don't lose customers because the work isn't good. They lose them in the gap between someone reaching out and someone following up. Here are the four places it usually happens.

📞
Missed calls that never get a reply. You're on a roof or under a sink. The call rings out — and most people just call the next business instead of leaving a voicemail.
📝
Quote requests that sit too long. A form comes in at 6pm. By the time it's answered the next morning, they've already booked someone who replied first.
⏱️
Slow first response. Speed wins local jobs. The business that answers in minutes usually gets the estimate — not the one with the nicest truck.
🔁
No follow-up, and no reviews asked for. Most jobs are lost to silence, not to price — and the reviews that would win the next job never get requested.
HOW LOLA OS CLOSES THE GAPS

Lola OS is the system that runs in the background so these don't slip: missed-call text-back replies the second you can't pick up, automated text and email follow-up keeps every new lead warm, and an automatic review request goes out after the job. It won't promise you a number of leads — no honest system can — but it makes sure the ones you already get don't fall through the cracks.
